Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay for both the $500 install and the $99/month plan?

No. They're separate choices. The $500 install buys you the chatbot, installed and working on your site — full stop. The $99/month Care Plan is an optional add-on if you'd like us to host it, monitor uptime, and make monthly updates for you. Plenty of clients take the install on its own.

How fast can my chatbot be live?

Usually within a few days of ordering. We customize it to your business, send it to you for approval, then install it on your website.

Do I need to give you my website login?

No. We work with a simple install snippet or coordinate with whoever manages your site — you never hand over passwords or logins.

What does the AI receptionist do that a chatbot doesn't?

The chatbot lives on your website and handles visitors there. The AI receptionist answers your actual phone calls, 24/7 — it talks with callers, books the job details, texts back anyone you missed, and asks finished customers for Google reviews. Many shops use both. See the AI Receptionist page for pricing.

Can I see the chatbot before I buy?

Yes — there's a working demo on a sample plumbing company's page. Try it here: chatbot demo. Ask it about hours, pricing, and services, then tap its quote button to see how a lead reaches the owner.

Are there contracts or cancellation fees?

No long-term contracts. The monthly services (Care Plan and AI Receptionist) are month-to-month and cancel anytime. The install and the DIY template are one-time purchases.

Who do you work with?

Small service businesses across Utah, especially the Salt Lake Valley — plumbers, HVAC companies, roofers, electricians, dentists, and similar trades. We're based in West Jordan, Utah.
