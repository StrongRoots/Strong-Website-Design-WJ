Frequently asked questions

What exactly does the free missed-call audit show?

We look at your own recent call activity and count how many calls went unanswered — after hours, during jobs, at lunch. Ten minutes, your real numbers, no obligation. If the numbers say you're not missing calls, we'll tell you that too.

Will callers know it's an AI?

It answers in a natural, helpful voice and handles the conversation like a trained receptionist: greeting your callers with your business name, taking details, and booking the job. It's upfront about being your virtual receptionist when asked.

How fast can it go live?

Within days of signing up. We build and configure your receptionist around your services and service area, then test it with you before it takes a single real call.

Do I keep my existing business number?

Yes — in most setups your number stays yours and callers notice no change, other than always getting an answer.
